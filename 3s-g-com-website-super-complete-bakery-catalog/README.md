# 3s G Com — Super Complete Bakery Catalog Edition

Rebuilt as a coherent artisan bakery, café, cakes, catering and wholesale website.

## Main improvements
- 47-item searchable catalog
- Separate Bread, Pastry, Cookies & Bars, Cakes, Savory, Coffee and Catering categories
- Dedicated preorder flow
- Dedicated custom cake request
- Catering inquiry
- Wholesale inquiry
- Bake schedule and availability messaging
- Allergen information
- Stronger Visit, FAQ, Journal, Careers, Press and Contact pages
- Responsive design, dark mode, saved items and catalog filters

## Verify before production
All prices, recipes, allergens, lead times, address, hours, availability and contact details are prototype/demo content and must be verified before launch.
