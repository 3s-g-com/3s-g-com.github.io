ALLERGEN INFORMATION

A working bakery handles common allergens every day.

This page is informational only. Final recipes and handling practices must be verified before launch.

Wheat / gluten

Most products in this prototype contain wheat. Cross-contact is expected in a conventional bakery.

Milk

Butter, milk, cream and cheese are used throughout pastry, cakes and café items.

Egg

Common in pastry, cakes, breakfast items and enriched doughs.

Nuts

Almonds and walnuts appear in sample products; cross-contact may occur.

Soy / sesame

May appear in ingredients or supplier products. Verify labels and recipes.

Ask before ordering

If an allergy is medically significant, confirm directly with the bakery rather than relying on website copy.
