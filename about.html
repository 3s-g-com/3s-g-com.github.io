About

Inside 3s G Com

3s G Com is a bakery in Chicago, IL. We bake through the night so the case is full when the door opens—sourdough, viennoiserie, and celebration cakes. We keep hours, offerings, and how to reach us clear so your visit feels straightforward.

Who we are

Find us at 401 W Division St, Chicago, IL. Call +1 (312) 555-0177 or write to support@3sgcom.com. We reply during business hours with clear next steps.
