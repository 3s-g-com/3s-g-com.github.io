## About 3s G Com

**3s G Com** is a **Bakery** in **Chicago** — We bake through the night so the case is full when the door opens—sourdough, viennoiserie, and celebration cakes.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

## Why we exist

3s G Com exists to make bakery feel personal in Chicago — clear pricing, honest recommendations, and a team that remembers why you came back.

- Rooted in Chicago with regulars who know our name
- Offerings that match what we publish online
- Support you can reach during real hours
- A space built for repeat visits, not one-offs
